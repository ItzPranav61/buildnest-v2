## Sponsor And Collaboration Positioning

Start with collaboration first. The best early pitch is not "buy a placement"; it is "let us help students discover your opportunity, and let us prove whether BuildNest can send useful awareness."

Sponsored featured placement should come later, after an organizer or community shows interest and understands the value. A paid placement should only be pitched when there is a clear opportunity, a trusted organizer, and a useful student-facing listing.

Recommended order:

1. Offer a free or lightweight collaboration.
2. Track interest, clicks, referrals, or community response.
3. Share a simple proof summary.
4. Pitch sponsored featured placement only if there is real interest.

## What Not To Claim

- Do not claim huge traffic.
- Do not claim paid sponsors yet.
- Do not claim guaranteed applicants.
- Do not exaggerate the CodeStorm partnership.
- Do not imply BuildNest is officially affiliated with a program unless that program has clearly confirmed it.
- Do not use fake scale, fake community numbers, or inflated referral claims.
