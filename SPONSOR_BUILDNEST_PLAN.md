# Sponsor BuildNest Plan

## Goal

Run a simple sponsorship experiment to recover basic BuildNest costs such as domain and hosting, while validating whether communities, organizers, or small teams will pay for featured opportunity placement.

This plan does not include payment implementation, Razorpay, or app changes.

## 1. Offer Positioning

Offer name:

**Featured Opportunity Placement**

Positioning:

BuildNest helps student builders discover internships, hackathons, fellowships, open-source programs, and builder tracks. A sponsored placement gives trusted organizers a clearer way to reach students while keeping BuildNest curated and review-first.

## 2. Target Customers

Potential sponsors:

- Hackathon organizers
- Coding communities
- College clubs
- Training institutes
- Student tech communities
- Small startups hiring interns
- Bootcamps
- Open-source program organizers

Best early prospects:

- Communities already posting opportunities on Discord, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or Instagram
- Hackathon organizers looking for registrations
- College clubs promoting flagship events
- Small startups hiring interns but struggling with reach

## 3. Pricing

Starter:

**₹499 for 7 days**

Monthly:

**₹999 for 30 days**

Pricing intent:

- Keep the experiment accessible for student communities and small organizers.
- Validate willingness to pay before building payment infrastructure.
- Recover small operating costs without turning BuildNest into an ad board.

## 4. What Sponsor Gets

Sponsor benefits:

- Featured listing on BuildNest
- External application link
- Discord/community mention
- Basic view/click report
- Priority placement
- Manual review before publishing

The sponsor does not get automatic publishing. Every sponsored opportunity still goes through quality review.

## 5. Rules

Sponsored listing rules:

- No fake opportunities
- No scammy internships
- No pay-to-apply listings
- No misleading “guaranteed internship” claims
- All sponsored listings must be clearly marked as Sponsored
- Admin can reject low-quality listings

Additional trust rules:

- Sponsored listings should still be useful to student builders.
- Payment does not guarantee approval.
- Reject anything that damages BuildNest credibility.
- Require a working external application or registration link.
- Require organizer name and source/contact proof.

## 6. Simple Validation Plan

Manual validation steps:

1. Create a sponsor message.
2. Make a list of 20 prospects.
3. Send 5-10 DMs first.
4. Track replies.
5. Accept UPI manually if someone agrees.
6. Publish manually after review.
7. Share a basic screenshot/report after the placement period.

Do not build a sponsor page until there is actual interest.

## 7. Success Criteria

Validated:

- 1 paid sponsor = validated

Worth building a sponsor page:

- 3 interested replies = worth building sponsor page

Needs revision:

- 0 replies = revise offer or audience

Suggested experiment window:

- Run outreach for 7-10 days.
- Send at least 20 thoughtful messages before judging the idea.

## 8. Outreach Message

Short DM:

```text
Hey, I’m building BuildNest, a curated opportunity board for student builders.

I’m testing a small Featured Opportunity Placement for hackathons, internships, fellowships, and community programs.

It’s ₹499 for 7 days or ₹999 for 30 days, includes a featured listing, external apply link, and a basic view/click report. Every listing is manually reviewed so the board stays clean.

Would you be interested in featuring your opportunity for students?
```

Shorter version:

```text
Hey, I run BuildNest, a curated student opportunity board.

I’m testing featured placements for hackathons, internships, and builder programs: ₹499 for 7 days or ₹999 for 30 days.

It includes a featured listing, apply link, community mention, and basic view/click report. Interested in trying it for your opportunity?
```

Follow-up:

```text
Quick follow-up on BuildNest featured placements. If you have an active opportunity for students, I can review it and feature it manually this week. No pressure, just testing if this is useful for organizers.
```

## 9. Tracking Sheet Columns

Use these columns:

- name
- type
- contact
- date messaged
- reply
- status
- notes

Suggested statuses:

- not contacted
- messaged
- interested
- paid
- rejected
- no reply
- follow-up sent

## 10. Risks

Risks:

- Low traffic
- Trust issues
- Poor sponsor quality
- Spammy listings
- Damaging user trust

Mitigations:

- Be transparent that this is an early experiment.
- Mark sponsored listings clearly.
- Reject low-quality listings even if they pay.
- Keep manual review strict.
- Share simple, honest reports instead of inflated metrics.
- Start with trusted communities and organizers first.

## 11. Recommendation

Recommendation:

**Sell manually first. Do not build the sponsor page yet.**

Reason:

The biggest unknown is not payment implementation. The biggest unknown is whether organizers will pay for BuildNest placement at current traffic and trust levels. Manual selling is faster, cheaper, and gives better learning.

Build the sponsor page only after:

- At least 1 paid sponsor, or
- 3 interested replies from credible organizers

## Plan Summary

BuildNest should test **Featured Opportunity Placement** manually with ₹499 and ₹999 options. The offer should stay review-first, clearly sponsored, and strict about quality. Payment can happen manually through UPI during validation.

## Next Action

Create a prospect list of 20 communities, clubs, hackathon organizers, and small startups. Send the first 5-10 DMs using the short outreach message, then track replies in a simple sheet.
